About Me

Hi, I am Wyatt. I am a data scientist who is passionate about playing the piano, running trails, and watching international films. I believe data work should start with questions, not tools. Too often, teams say they want to be data-driven when what they really need is better framing. My strength is slowing the problem down, clarifying the decision at stake, and turning messy information into focused action. I have worked across marketing, analytics, and operations, translating client and business goals into practical strategies that actually perform. I enjoy translating real business problems into analytics people can understand and use, whether in a classroom, a workshop, or a working session. I care deeply about efficiency. In one role, I compressed months of manual reporting into a few hours by rebuilding workflows and reporting systems, improving both accuracy and trust in the data. Across roles, the pattern is consistent. I connect data to decisions, reduce unnecessary complexity, and build systems people want to rely on. I value strong teams, mentorship, and continuous learning, and I enjoy sharing what I know while learning from others. If you have questions about your business and are trying to make better decisions, I would love to connect.
