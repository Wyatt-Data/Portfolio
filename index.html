About Me

I am a data scientist who starts with questions, not dashboards. In a world saturated with data, the real constraint is focus. I care about framing the right questions, then building practical models and tools that turn answers into action. My work emphasizes efficiency. Removing redundant effort, automating what should not be manual, and creating clarity that supports better decisions. I value strong teams, thoughtful mentorship, and continuous learning, and I aim to contribute work that is useful, reliable, and grounded in real business needs. Outside of work, I play piano, run trails, practice yoga, and watch international films.

Core Values and Principles

I turn messy information into decisions people can actually use.
Not models for their own sake. Not dashboards no one checks. Work that survives contact with reality.

I optimize for signal, not spectacle.
Simple approaches, applied deliberately, outperform clever ones deployed thoughtlessly.

I start from the decision, then work backward.
If the output does not change an action, the analysis is unfinished.

I distrust false precision.
Clear assumptions beat fragile certainty. Knowing the limits matters as much as the result.

I treat data as a tool, not an answer.
The value is not in collection or visualization. It is in judgment.
